Trade Monitor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026
Publisher: Nikki Lodhi

About Trade Monitor

Trade Monitor provides general market information, charts, indicator signals and alerts for supported instruments, including UKOUSD, XAUUSD, BTCUSD and USOUSD. It is an informational tool; it does not connect to brokerage accounts, execute trades, or provide personalized financial advice.

Information processed by the app

You do not need to create an account or enter your name, email address, payment details or brokerage credentials. The app does not send your signal history to a server operated by Nikki Lodhi. Signal history, monitoring preferences, notification preferences, and the most recent 10 entry-notification records are stored locally on your device. On Android, app-data backup is disabled. On Apple devices, local app data is managed by the operating system and may be included in device backups according to your device settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes its local app data.

While the Android app is open in the foreground, it sends a random temporary session ID to the Trade Monitor service about every 30 seconds so the Home screen can show an approximate count of active app sessions. The ID is generated for that app session, is not linked to an account or device identity, and is kept only in server memory; inactive sessions expire after about 75 seconds and server restarts clear the count. The service processes the network connection, including IP address, to return the count. No signal or notification history is sent for this feature.

If you enable notifications and start a pair on iOS, the app sends your APNs device token, selected pair, and subscription expiry to the Trade Monitor push service hosted by Cloudflare so it can deliver entry alerts while the app is in the background. The service retains this subscription only while monitoring is active (up to 30 minutes for Free/Lite, or until you stop for Max). Entry alerts include the pair, direction, entry price, and signal time. Exit events do not send notifications. You can disable notifications per pair in the app; stopping a pair removes its subscription.

Market data — Bitget

When market data is loaded or monitoring is active, the app requests public ticker and candle data from Bitget. For Apple push alerts, the Trade Monitor push service also requests one-minute candle data from Bitget while a pair has an active subscription. The requests include market symbols and chart parameters such as interval and requested time range. Bitget and network providers necessarily receive your IP address and technical connection information with these requests and may infer your approximate location. The app does not send your locally stored signal history or brokerage credentials to Bitget. Bitget handles information it receives under its own Privacy Notice.

Country check on Apple devices

When you start monitoring on the iOS or macOS version, the app checks the apparent country of your internet connection by requesting the country code from ipapi.co. This request necessarily exposes your IP address and technical connection information to ipapi.co. The app uses the returned country code to decide whether to show its India VPN prompt; it does not store the IP address or use this check to identify you. This country check is not used by the Android version. See ipapi.co’s privacy policy for its handling of request data.

Advertising

The Android free version can show a Unity LevelPlay interstitial when a monitoring session starts. If you choose to enable Offers, the Unity Offerwall (Tapjoy) SDK connects only after you accept its in-app disclosure. Unity/Tapjoy may process your advertising ID, IP address, app/device information, and offer views or interactions to provide, personalize, measure, and protect offers. You can decline the Offerwall and continue using the rest of the app. Unity and enabled advertising networks handle information under their own privacy policies. Lite and Max remove ads. The iOS version may also display Google AdMob interstitial ads.

Read the Tapjoy Privacy Policy. Tapjoy privacy requests can be submitted at its privacy request form or by emailing tapjoy-privacy-request@unity3d.com.

In-app purchases

iOS Lite and Max purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit; Android Lite and Max purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. The app receives purchase entitlement status to provide the selected features; Lite removes ads, and Max removes ads and enables simultaneous pair monitoring. The app does not receive or store your full payment card details. Apple and Google handle purchase and payment information under their own privacy policies.

Analytics and sale of data

The app does not use a first-party analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDK. When you enable Offers, Unity/Tapjoy measures Offerwall views and interactions as described above. Nikki Lodhi does not sell personal information collected through the app.

Permissions and controls

Internet access is used for market data, Unity LevelPlay ads on Android, and the optional Unity Offerwall after you enable Offers; on Apple devices it may also be used for the country-code check described above and Google AdMob ads. Notification permission is optional and controls whether local and remote entry signal notifications can be shown. You can enable or disable alerts separately for each pair in the iOS Home screen. The app does not request precise device location. You can choose Not now in the Offerwall disclosure and continue without connecting the Offerwall SDK, stop monitoring in the app, and change notification permission in your device settings.

Children

Trade Monitor is a general market-information app and is not designed to collect personal information from children. It does not require an account or ask users to provide personal details.

Data retention and policy changes

Signal history, notification history, and preferences remain in local app data until cleared or the app is uninstalled. Push subscriptions are retained by the Trade Monitor push service only while active and are removed when monitoring is stopped or the subscription expires. Bitget, ipapi.co, Unity/Tapjoy, Google, Apple, and Cloudflare determine their own retention and processing practices for information they receive, as described in their respective privacy notices. This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The current policy will remain available at this URL.
