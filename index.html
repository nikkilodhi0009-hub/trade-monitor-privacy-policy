Trade Monitor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026
Publisher: Nikki Lodhi

About Trade Monitor

Trade Monitor provides general market information, charts, indicator signals and alerts for UKOUSD, XAUUSD, BTCUSD and USOUSD. It is informational only. It does not connect to brokerage accounts, execute trades, or provide personalized financial advice.

Information stored on your device

You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, payment details or brokerage credentials. Signal history, monitoring preferences, notification preferences, and up to 10 recent entry-notification records are stored locally on your device. On Apple devices, local app data is managed by iOS and may be included in backups according to your device settings. Removing the app removes its local data. The app does not upload this local signal or notification history to the push service.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications and start monitoring a pair on iOS, Apple provides the app with an APNs device token. The app sends this token, the selected pair, the app identifier, notification environment, and subscription expiration to the Trade Monitor push service hosted by Cloudflare. The service stores the token while the pair subscription is active so it can send entry alerts while the app is in the background. Stopping monitoring removes that pair subscription; free and Lite subscriptions expire after 30 minutes. When there are no active pair subscriptions, the app asks the service to remove the token registration.

Push alerts contain the pair, direction, entry price and signal time. The current app sends push notifications for entries; it does not send exit notifications. Apple and Cloudflare process technical information needed to deliver the notification under their own privacy terms.

Market data

When you load market data or monitor a pair, Trade Monitor requests public ticker and candle data from Bitget. To detect signals for active iOS push subscriptions, the Trade Monitor push service also requests one-minute candle data from Bitget. Requests include market symbols and chart parameters. Bitget, Cloudflare and your network provider may receive IP addresses and technical connection information and may infer an approximate location from an IP address. The app does not send your local signal history or brokerage credentials to Bitget. See Bitget's Privacy Notice.

Advertising

The current iOS build uses Google's official test application and interstitial ad unit IDs during testing. Google Mobile Ads may process technical information such as IP address, device and app information, ad request diagnostics, and interactions with test ads to provide and troubleshoot the SDK. The app does not currently use an IP-country check or request precise device location. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob iOS data disclosure. Production advertising configuration may change in a later release, and this policy will be updated if the data practices change.

In-app purchases and Max codes

iOS Lite and Max purchases, when offered, are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The app receives purchase entitlement status to provide the purchased features; it does not receive or store full payment-card details. Apple's handling of payment information is described in its privacy policy.

If you redeem a Max access code, the app sends the code and an app-generated random device identifier to the Trade Monitor service. The service stores a SHA-256 hash of the code, the device identifier, and activation/update times to validate and assign the code to one device at a time. The raw code and device identifier are kept in the device Keychain. Transferring a code to another device removes the prior device's entitlement when it next checks with the service.

Permissions, analytics and data sale

Notification permission is optional and controls whether signal notifications can be shown. You can enable or disable notifications for each pair in the app and change notification permission in iOS Settings. The app does not request precise device location. The app does not include an analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDK, and Nikki Lodhi does not sell personal information collected through the app.

Data retention and choices

Local signal history, notification history and preferences remain on your device until cleared or the app is removed. Push tokens are kept by the Trade Monitor push service only for active subscriptions and are removed when monitoring stops or the subscription expires. Bitget, Cloudflare, Google and Apple determine their own retention and processing practices for information they receive, as described in their privacy notices. You can stop monitoring at any time and disable notifications in iOS Settings.

Children

Trade Monitor is a general market-information app. It does not require an account or ask users to provide personal details.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The current version will remain available at this URL.
