Trade Monitor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026
Publisher: Nikki Lodhi

About Trade Monitor

Trade Monitor provides general market information, charts, indicator signals and alerts for supported instruments, including UKOUSD, XAUUSD, BTCUSD and USOUSD. It is an informational tool; it does not connect to brokerage accounts, execute trades, or provide personalized financial advice.

Information processed by the app

You do not need to create an account or enter your name, email address, payment details or brokerage credentials. The app does not send your signal history to a server operated by Nikki Lodhi. Signal history and monitoring preferences are stored locally on your device so the app can display them. On Android, app-data backup is disabled. On Apple devices, local app data is managed by the operating system and may be included in device backups according to your device settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes its local app data.

If you allow notifications, Trade Monitor can show monitoring and signal notifications on your device. These are generated locally by the app; it does not use a remote push-notification service.

Market data — Bitget

When market data is loaded or monitoring is active, the app requests public ticker and candle data from Bitget. The requests include market symbols and chart parameters such as interval and requested time range. Bitget and network providers necessarily receive your IP address and technical connection information with these requests and may infer your approximate location. The app does not send your locally stored signal history or brokerage credentials to Bitget. Bitget handles information it receives under its own Privacy Notice.

Country check on Apple devices

When you start monitoring on the iOS or macOS version, the app checks the apparent country of your internet connection by requesting the country code from ipapi.co. This request necessarily exposes your IP address and technical connection information to ipapi.co. The app uses the returned country code to decide whether to show its India VPN prompt; it does not store the IP address or use this check to identify you. This country check is not used by the Android version. See ipapi.co’s privacy policy for its handling of request data.

Advertising, analytics and sale of data

The current app versions do not display ads and do not include an advertising SDK, analytics SDK or third-party crash-reporting SDK. Nikki Lodhi does not sell personal information collected through the app.

Permissions and controls

Internet access is used for market data and, on Apple devices, the country-code check described above. Notification permission is optional and controls whether local monitoring and signal notifications can be shown. The app does not request precise device location. You can stop monitoring in the app and change notification permission in your device settings.

Children

Trade Monitor is a general market-information app and is not designed to collect personal information from children. It does not require an account or ask users to provide personal details.

Data retention and policy changes

Signal history and preferences remain in local app data until cleared or the app is uninstalled. Bitget and ipapi.co determine their own retention and processing practices for requests they receive, as described in their respective privacy notices. This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The current policy will remain available at this URL.
