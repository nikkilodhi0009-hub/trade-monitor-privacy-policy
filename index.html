Trade Monitor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026
Publisher: Nikki Lodhi

About the app

Trade Monitor provides general Ichimoku indicator information and crossover alerts for UKOUSD. It does not connect to a brokerage account or place trades.

Information stored on your device

The app does not ask you to create an account or enter your name, email address, payment details, or brokerage credentials. Crossover history is stored locally on your Android device so it can appear when you reopen the app. Android app-data backup is disabled. The app does not upload this history to a Nikki Lodhi server. Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data removes this local history.

If you allow notifications and start monitoring, the app displays a persistent monitoring notification and on-device crossover alerts. The app does not use a remote push-notification server.

Market data provider

The app requests public UKOUSD candle data from Bitget's market-data API. Requests contain market and chart parameters such as the symbol, interval, and requested time range. As with ordinary internet requests, Bitget and network providers may receive your IP address and technical connection information. The app does not send your crossover history or account credentials to Bitget.

Bitget handles information it receives under its own Privacy Notice.

Advertising

This version of Trade Monitor does not display advertisements and does not include an advertising SDK. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before it is enabled.

Permissions

  • Internet: Used to request Bitget market data.
  • Notifications: Used for monitoring status and crossover alerts. You can change this permission in Android Settings.
  • Vibration: Used to signal a detected crossover when monitoring is active.
  • Location: The app does not request precise device location. Bitget may infer your approximate location from your IP address when it receives market-data requests.

Data retention and choices

Crossover history remains in local app data until you clear that data or uninstall the app. Bitget determines retention for information it collects under its own policy.

You may disable notifications in Android Settings or stop monitoring in the app. You may stop market-data requests by stopping monitoring and closing the app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at the policy URL shown on the Google Play product page.
