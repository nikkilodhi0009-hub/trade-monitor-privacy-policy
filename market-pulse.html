Market Pulse Privacy Policy and Support. Last updated: 31 October 2026. Publisher: Nikki Lodhi. Market Pulse shows public Bitget prices for UKOUSD, XAUUSD and USOUSD CFDs, plus BTCUSDT perpetual as BTCUSD. The app has no account, ads, analytics or crash reporting and does not collect names, emails, phone numbers, payment details or broker credentials. Starting a Live Activity sends its ActivityKit push token, chosen market and APNs environment to the publisher's service, which stores them to request price updates. The service sends the token and quote (symbol, price, change and time) to Apple Push Notification service; invalid tokens are removed. Hosting restarts may clear temporary records. The app and push service request public Bitget prices. Bitget, Render and Apple may receive network technical information. Provider privacy terms: https://www.bitget.com/terms/legal/360015150651 ; https://render.com/privacy ; https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ . Quotes and token records are retained temporarily for updates, until invalidation or storage clearing. No tracking, advertising, profiling or sale of personal information. Prices may be delayed. CFDs and perpetual futures are leveraged and risky. This app is not financial advice. Stop Live Activity in the app or device controls. Contact and support: lodhiboys74@gmail.com. Market Pulse is not designed to collect personal information from children. Policy may change with app features.
