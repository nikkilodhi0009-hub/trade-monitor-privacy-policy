Market Pulse Privacy Policy and Support

Last updated: 31 October 2026
Publisher: Nikki Lodhi

About Market Pulse

Market Pulse displays public market quotes for UKOUSD, XAUUSD, BTCUSD, and USOUSD. UKOUSD, XAUUSD, and USOUSD use Bitget CFD market data; BTCUSD displays the BTCUSDT perpetual market. The app is informational. It does not connect to brokerage accounts, place orders, or provide personalized investment advice.

Information the app processes

Market Pulse does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, payment details, or brokerage credentials. It does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

When you start a Live Activity, iOS provides an ActivityKit push token. The app sends that token, your selected market symbol, and whether the app uses the development or production APNs environment to the Market Pulse push service operated by Nikki Lodhi. The service stores the token and selected market so it can request price updates for your Live Activity. It does not receive an account name or other profile details from the app.

The service sends the token and quote payload (market symbol, price, percentage change, and update time) to Apple Push Notification service to deliver Live Activity updates. The service removes tokens when APNs reports them as invalid. Temporary registration files may be cleared when the hosting service restarts. You can stop a Live Activity from the app or your device controls.

Market data and network providers

When the app is open, it requests public ticker data from Bitget. The background push service also requests Bitget quotes to prepare Live Activity updates. These requests include the relevant market symbols. Bitget, Render (the push-service host), Apple, and network providers may receive IP addresses and technical connection information as part of delivering these services. The app does not send locally displayed prices or a personal profile to Bitget. Each provider handles information it receives under its own privacy terms: Bitget Privacy Notice, Render Privacy Policy, and Apple Privacy Policy.

Data use and retention

Market quotes are held temporarily by the push service to supply updates. Live Activity token records are kept for delivery until APNs invalidates the token or the service storage is cleared. The app does not use data for tracking, advertising, or user profiling, and Nikki Lodhi does not sell personal information.

Financial information

Prices and changes can be delayed, unavailable, or different from prices offered by a broker. CFDs and perpetual futures are complex, leveraged products. Market Pulse is not financial advice. Check current market information and consider the risks before making financial decisions.
